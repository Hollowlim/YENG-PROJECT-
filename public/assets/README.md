# Sample imagery

The campaign and product photos here were generated with OpenAI Image Gen for this CROWNNETIC sample. They are illustrative, not photographs of actual inventory. Original generated files were preserved in `/workspace/generated_images`; these local copies are used by the website. Replace them with your real campaign and product photography before selling these items.

The `logo-black.png` wordmark was corrected from the supplied logo with Image Gen to read **CROWNNETIC**, adding the second N while retaining the geometric lettering and stylized R. Its transparent artwork feeds the header, footer and MetallicPaint treatment.
